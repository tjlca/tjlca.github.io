In the classroom

Teaching

I want students to leave my courses with three durable habits: to frame engineering problems as part of larger socio-environmental systems, to interrogate data quantitatively with confidence, and to iterate designs until they balance performance with planetary limits.

Quantitative Sustainability

A planned two-module course: sustainability analysis (systems thinking, planetary boundaries, process-based & hybrid LCA on real datasets) and sustainable engineering design (GAMS, Python, open-source LCA databases, multi-objective optimization).
