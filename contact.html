Work With SEED

Let's build sustainable systems together

Are you interested in the future of sustainable engineering? The SEED Group welcomes collaboration with universities, national laboratories, agencies, and industry to design and evaluate low-carbon energy and materials systems. We welcome inquiries from partners exploring life cycle assessment, techno-economic analysis, circular economy, and AI-driven sustainability analytics.

We are also actively recruiting motivated PhD and MS students and welcome postdoc and undergraduate researchers passionate about sustainable systems. If you would like to join the team or partner with us, please reach out — we look forward to connecting.
