About

A "Process-to-Planet" approach to sustainability.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Gene and Linda Voiland School of Chemical Engineering and Bioengineering at Washington State University Tri-Cities, with a joint appointment at the Institute for Northwest Energy Futures (INEF), in the heart of the Columbia River energy corridor next to Pacific Northwest National Laboratory.

Through the TERRA Lab — Techno-Ecological Research for Resilient Anthropogenic Systems — my group develops computational frameworks, open-source tools, and systems models that quantify the full environmental, economic, and social cost of human energy and industrial systems.

Before joining WSU, I was a Senior Research Scientist at the U.S. DOE National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL), a postdoctoral fellow at the MIT Energy Initiative, and earned my PhD in Chemical Engineering at The Ohio State University under Prof. Bhavik R. Bakshi. My work spans life cycle assessment, techno-ecological synergy, circular economy modeling, and AI-driven sustainability analytics.

What I work on

Research at every scale

Engineering alone — optimizing within fixed system boundaries — is no longer enough. TERRA Lab pushes those boundaries outward to include ecological systems, economic equilibrium, and AI-driven analytics within engineering design itself.

Circularity

Circular Economy & Critical Materials

Dynamic material-flow and agent-based models (CELAVI, 4P) for wind-blade, solar-PV, battery, and plastics circularity — optimizing end-of-life pathways by economic and environmental criteria.

Methods

Life Cycle Assessment & Absolute Sustainability

Process-based, hybrid, prospective, and dynamic LCA — coupled with techno-ecological synergy and planetary boundaries to measure whether a system is genuinely sustainable, not just relatively better.

Analytics

AI & Integrated Assessment Modeling

LLM- and RAG-powered LCA automation (LiAISON), integrated assessment model coupling (GCAM, IMAGE), and capacity-expansion-aware emission factors for a decarbonizing grid.
