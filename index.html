About Me

Welcome to my site!

I am currently working as a researcher at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL). My work focuses on exploring innovative solutions in renewable energy, sustainability, and advanced technologies. This website contains an overview of my research experience, expertise, and education.

Feel free to look around and get in touch if you have any questions.

Research Interests

Circular Economy of Energy Systems

Leading efforts to expand circular economy methods to develop integrated frameworks for quantifying and visualizing the environmental impacts of energy systems, with a focus on geospatially and temporally explicit scenario analyses, material flows, and sustainable technology pathways.

Environmental Impact & Life Cycle Assessment

Conducting and supporting LCA studies on building materials, manufacturing processes, and energy technologies, while developing code-based LCA tools and integrating prospective LCA with system models for robust sustainability evaluations.

Technoeconomic & Policy Analysis

Applying integrated assessment models, technoeconomic analyses, and advanced optimization techniques to guide policy development, assess resource utilization, and evaluate market-driven solutions for circular economy and low-carbon energy transitions.
