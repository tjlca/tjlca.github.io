About the Lab

What is SEED?

The Sustainable Engineering and Ecological Design (SEED) Group at WSU Tri-Cities builds computational frameworks that quantify the full life-cycle cost of energy and industrial systems — from the molecular scale of a chemical process to the planetary scale of ecosystem boundaries.

Research

Pioneering life cycle assessment, techno-economic analysis, and AI-driven analytics for sustainable energy and materials systems.

Education

Training the next generation of engineers in quantitative sustainability, systems thinking, and open-source modeling.

Engagement & Policy

Sharing open, transparent analysis to inform equitable, evidence-based decarbonization across the Pacific Northwest.

Research Topics

An overview of the areas SEED is building its program around, applied to Pacific-Northwest energy and industrial systems.

Research topics

Years in sustainable systems

Life Cycle & Techno-Economic Assessment

Process-based, hybrid, prospective, and dynamic LCA coupled with techno-economic analysis and absolute-sustainability benchmarks.

Circular Economy & Critical Materials

Dynamic material-flow and agent-based modeling for wind blades, solar PV, batteries, and plastics, and critical-mineral recovery.

AI & Prospective LCA

LLM/RAG automation of life-cycle inventories and integrated-assessment coupling so impacts evolve as technologies and the grid change.

Hydrogen & Low-Carbon Fuels

LCA and techno-economic assessment of hydrogen, sustainable aviation fuel, and green-hydrogen pathways for chemicals and transport.

Advanced Nuclear & Grid Decarbonization

Life-cycle and techno-economic assessment of Small Modular Reactors and capacity-expansion modeling for the regional grid.

Food–Energy–Water Nexus

Agrivoltaics, agricultural electrification, and biomass supply-chain sustainability across the Columbia Basin.
