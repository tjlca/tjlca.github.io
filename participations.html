Presentations at AIChE, FOCAPD, PSE, ISIE, ACLCA, and Brightcon — and the honors that have recognized this work along the way.

Selected talks

Workshops & Conferences

  • 2024Brightcon 2024 · Hamburg, Germany
    Prospective impact analysis combining integrated assessment modeling and LCA for alternative industrial heat sources.
  • 2023ISIE 2023 · Leiden, Netherlands
    A prospective LCA framework for emerging technologies in a dynamic system context; LiAISON — LCA Integration into Scalable Open-source Numerical models for low-carbon technologies.
  • 2021ACLCA 2021 · Virtual
    Attributional to Consequential Life Cycle Assessment — steps toward a unified framework.
  • 2019AIChE Annual Meeting 2019 · Orlando, FL
    Designing sustainable biofuel supply chains incorporating spatial variability of nutrient runoff and ecosystem services.
  • 2019FOCAPD 2019 · Copper Mountain, CO
    Integrating market models and price effects in a multiscale Sustainable Process Design framework.
  • 2018AIChE Annual Meeting 2018 · Pittsburgh, PA
    Electricity LCI data for the Federal LCA Commons; a framework for multiscale consequential LCA; spatial variability of ecosystem services in supply-chain design; LCA model design under uncertainty and complexity.
  • 2018LCA XVIII 2018 · Fort Collins, CO
    Toward systematic design of LCA models by accounting for uncertainty and network complexity.
  • 2018PSE 2018 · San Diego, CA
    Including ecosystem services in sustainable process design across multiple spatial scales.
  • 2017AIChE Annual Meeting 2017 · Minneapolis, MN
    Life cycle optimization with ecosystems as unit operations.
  • 2016AIChE Annual Meeting 2016 · San Francisco, CA
    Process-to-Planet framework for sustainable design: a systematic approach to multiscale modeling and multi-objective optimization.
