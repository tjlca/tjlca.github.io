Our Approach

Process to Planet

Every engineering design decision sends consequences rippling outward — from the process floor to the planetary atmosphere. SEED builds computational frameworks that connect molecular thermodynamics, unit operations, supply chains, regional economies, and global ecosystems within a single, quantitative structure.

A defining idea in this work is reframing life cycle assessment through the lens of optimization: by introducing decision variables directly into the LCA matrices, the same structure becomes a design tool that can guide process, technology, and supply-chain choices — rather than an after-the-fact audit.

Research Agenda

Seven Research Topics

1 Life Cycle & Techno-Economic Assessment

  • Process-based, hybrid, prospective, and dynamic LCA
  • Integrated LCA–TEA for energy and industrial systems
  • Absolute sustainability and planetary-boundary benchmarking
  • Regionalized life cycle impact assessment for North America

2 Circular Economy & Critical Materials

  • Dynamic material-flow and agent-based modeling
  • End-of-life pathways for wind blades, solar PV, and batteries
  • Critical-mineral recovery and industrial symbiosis
  • Circular economy for plastics under state policy

3 AI & Integrated Assessment Modeling

  • LLM/RAG automation of life-cycle inventories
  • Prospective LCA coupled with integrated assessment models
  • Physics-informed surrogate models for rapid assessment
  • Capacity-expansion-aware, decarbonizing emission factors

4 Hydrogen & Low-Carbon Fuels

  • LCA & TEA of low-carbon and green hydrogen pathways
  • Sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) supply chains
  • Hydrogen for chemicals, fertilizer, and heavy transport

5 Advanced Nuclear & Grid Decarbonization

  • Life-cycle and techno-economic assessment of Small Modular Reactors
  • Capacity-expansion modeling for the regional grid through 2050
  • Energy equity and justice in grid transitions

6 Food–Energy–Water Nexus

  • Agrivoltaics LCA & techno-economic analysis
  • Agricultural electrification and irrigation-canal solar
  • Biomass and bioenergy supply-chain sustainability

7 Process Systems Engineering & Digital Twins

  • AI-integrated process design for low-carbon chemical manufacturing
  • Process-to-Planet analysis for regional industries (pulp/paper, aluminum, food, semiconductors)
  • Digital twins coupling real-time process data with dynamic LCA
